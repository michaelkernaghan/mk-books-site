Royal Army Medical Corps Training, 1911

War Office (Great Britain) · Harrison and Sons, London, 1911

Original War Office training manual, 'Royal Army Medical Corps Training, 1911' (W.O. 40/1905), printed for His Majesty's Stationery Office by Harrison and Sons, London; this copy a 1914 reprint of the 1911 manual. Crown Copyright. Issued by command of the Army Council, 14th July 1911. Green cloth boards with a dark spine panel and gilt lettering. A piece of Edwardian / pre-First World War British military medicine - field medical organisation, bearer and stretcher drill, first aid, and RAMC training as taught on the eve of the Great War.
