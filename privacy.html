Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 April 2026

What this website collects

Nothing. This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies. We do not operate any forms or accounts on this site, and we do not log visitor behaviour.

Email correspondence

If you contact us by email using the address on our Contact page, we use your message and email address solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not add correspondents to any mailing list.

Purchases on AbeBooks

Every purchase from MK Books is completed on AbeBooks. AbeBooks collects and processes the personal and payment information required to fulfil an order. That information is governed by AbeBooks' privacy policy, not this one.

When AbeBooks provides us with a shipping address and name in order to fulfil an order, we use that information only to pack and ship the book, and we retain it for the period required for tax and record-keeping purposes in British Columbia, Canada.

Third parties

This site is hosted on Netlify. Netlify may log incoming requests for the purposes of operating its service; see Netlify's privacy policy for details. We do not receive or process those logs.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the email address on our Contact page.
