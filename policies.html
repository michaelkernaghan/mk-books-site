Returns & Refunds

All sales are fulfilled through AbeBooks and are subject to the AbeBooks Buyer Guarantee. Returns and refunds are handled per AbeBooks' standard policy. In the usual case:

  • If a book is materially different from its listed description, you may request a return through AbeBooks.
  • If a book does not arrive, the AbeBooks Buyer Guarantee provides a full refund.
  • Return shipping and restocking terms follow AbeBooks' current policy at the time of the transaction.

If you have a question before or after purchase, contact us at the address on our contact page and we will respond within two business days.

Shipping

Orders placed through our AbeBooks storefront are packed and shipped within two business days. Shipping options, rates, carriers, and delivery estimates are set by AbeBooks at checkout; we do not operate a separate shipping channel.

We ship from Mission, British Columbia, Canada. AbeBooks restricts the destinations we offer; please consult the storefront for current availability.
